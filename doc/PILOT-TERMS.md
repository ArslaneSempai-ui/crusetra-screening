# Pilot annex: outsourcing clauses, a draft for your counsel

A draft of the clauses a regulated firm usually needs before running a tool in a pilot. It
is not legal advice and it names no price; it is meant to be read by your counsel and ours,
and cut to what your policy requires.

1. **Scope.** A time-boxed evaluation of one tool on the customer's own data, on the
   customer's machines, for a stated number of days, with written success criteria agreed
   before the start (who reproduces a decision, in how many minutes, with what access, in
   front of whom).
2. **No replacement of controls.** During the pilot the tool replaces no existing control,
   process or decision. Every candidate it raises is read by the customer's staff; nothing is
   cleared or blocked by the tool alone.
3. **Data.** The customer's files never leave the customer's machines. The provider receives
   nothing unless the customer chooses to send it, and then keeps no copy after delivery. The
   tool needs no personal data beyond what a counterparty file already contains.
4. **Confidentiality.** Mutual. The provider learns nothing about the customer's counterparties
   through the tool; what the customer chooses to share in conversation stays confidential.
5. **Intellectual property.** The tool stays the provider's, under its published licence. Each
   report is the customer's document.
6. **Service levels.** None during the pilot. Support is by written contact within stated
   hours.
7. **Liability.** To be set by counsel; the tool is not legal advice and does not establish a
   match.
8. **Sub-outsourcing.** None. The provider uses no sub-processor and no third-party service in
   delivering the tool.
9. **Audit and inspection.** The source is readable, the network boundary is a test, and the
   customer may inspect both at any time.
10. **Regulatory responsibility.** The customer remains responsible for its screening and its
    decisions. The pilot creates no reliance on the provider.
11. **Termination.** At will by either party, with the customer keeping its reports.
12. **References.** The provider makes no use of the customer's name or logo without a
    separate, written consent, never as a condition of the pilot or the licence.
